# Make the project your own: hands-on challenges

The completed assessment provides a worked example. To demonstrate **your** analysis, try these changes on a separate branch or copy. Do not claim you've completed them until you have.

1. **Reperform manually.** Open the evidence CSVs without reading the supplied working paper. Reconcile 12 accounts and write your own findings memo. Compare results afterward.
2. **Challenge population completeness.** Add a synthetic third system, "Payroll Portal", to the scoping memo but do not include an export. What can you still conclude? What can't you conclude?
3. **Introduce conflicting evidence.** Change a snapshot status to `ACTIVE` for an account with a disable event. Run the script and explain what corroborating evidence you would request.
4. **Test a remediation cycle.** Invent a *new, distinct* November termination population and determine whether the revised CRM workflow worked. Keep the October findings unchanged.
5. **Extend to least privilege (AC-6).** Add a fictional roles/entitlements register, business approvals and user permissions. Test one job-transfer case for lingering privilege, separately from the termination test.
6. **Explain the 24-hour threshold.** Tell a reader why that number comes from Harborline's **fictional policy**, not automatically from NIST.

**Self-review checklist:** Separate fact from inference; name the control criterion; validate populations; show timestamps; explain evidence gaps; rate risk cautiously; propose measurable, verifiable corrective actions.
